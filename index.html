Payment & Conditions

💳
Payment Terms

📋
Order Lead Time

Bulk orders (including packaging and labelling): 1–2 working days from order confirmation, depending on product and quantity.

Export Documents
Every shipment includes the standard document set listed in our Importer's Guide, plus any destination-specific certificates required.
📦
Packaging Standards
Default: food-grade multi-layer PP/HDPE bags (25 kg) packed in corrugated cartons. Vacuum-sealed and moisture-proof packing, as well as private label and retail packs, are available on request.
🔬
Quality Assurance
Every batch is tested at NABL-accredited labs for pesticide residues, aflatoxins, moisture content, ash content and heavy metals. The COA is shared before shipment.
🛡️
Disputes & Returns
Quality claims must be raised within 14 days of port arrival, with a report from an ISO/IEC 17025-accredited lab in the destination country. Claims are handled case by case, with partial credit or a replacement shipment.
