🚢 Incoterms We Offer
FOB — Free On Board
Risk transfers at Indian port. Best for experienced importers with their own freight forwarder.
CIF — Cost Insurance Freight
We cover freight & insurance to destination port. Recommended for new importers.
DAP — Delivered At Place
We deliver to your warehouse. Buyer handles import duties at destination.
Trade Terms

Payment & Conditions

Standard export trading terms we follow. All terms are negotiable for long-term buyers and regular importers.

💳
Payment Terms
New buyers: 50% advance (T/T wire transfer) + 50% against copy of B/L.

Repeat buyers: 30% advance + 70% against B/L, or Letter of Credit (LC) at sight.

Accepted: T/T (SWIFT), LC at sight, Western Union for sample payments.
📋
Order Lead Time
Samples: Dispatched within 3–5 working days of payment.

Bulk orders: 15–25 working days from order confirmation, depending on product and quantity.

Custom packaging / private label: Add 7–10 days for artwork and printing.
🧾
Export Documents
Standard shipment set includes: Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Bill of Lading, Certificate of Origin, Phytosanitary Certificate, NABL COA, FSSAI certificate, and any destination-specific certificates (Halal, Organic, EU MRL).
📦
Packaging Standards
Default: food-grade multi-layer PP/HDPE bags (25 kg), packed in corrugated cartons. Vacuum-sealed and moisture-proof on request.

Private label: Custom printed pouches and bags available from 200 kg+.
🔬
Quality Assurance
Every batch tested at NABL-accredited labs for: pesticide residues, aflatoxins, moisture content, ash content, and heavy metals. COA shared before shipment.

Pre-shipment inspection by third-party agencies available on buyer request.
🛡️
Dispute & Returns
Quality claims must be raised within 14 days of port arrival, with NABL lab report from destination country. Claims are handled case-by-case with partial credit or replacement shipment.

Marine insurance arranged on request (CIF & DAP terms include insurance).
